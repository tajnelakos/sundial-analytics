Company Overview & Strategy
Bricklane Data is a fast-growing, price-led challenger targeting mid-market banks and lenders across Europe with self-serve, API-first valuations. They position against us on speed and cost, framing explainability as a nice-to-have rather than a requirement — a frame that holds only until a risk or compliance stakeholder enters the deal.
Positioning Framework
They say
"The fastest, cheapest valuations in the market — get started in minutes, no sales call required."
Reality
Fast and cheap, yes — but the audit trail behind it is a confidence score with no comparable-level breakdown, and there's no dedicated compliance/audit-log feature. (Source: product comparison, unconfirmed independently — worth a live demo comparison before repeating as certain.)
We win because
We give you a valuation you can defend to an auditor, not just receive — comparable-level detail on every decision, not just a number.

Objection Handlers
"Bricklane is 15% cheaper."
That's fair, and worth factoring in — but ask what happens the first time an examiner questions a valuation. A cheaper number you can't explain ends up costing more in rework than it saved. Happy to show you the actual audit trail on one of your own properties so you can judge for yourself.
"They're faster to get started — self-serve, no sales call."
Speed to start is real, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. The question is speed to a defensible decision, not just speed to a number — that's where the audit trail earns its keep.
"Bricklane says their accuracy is comparable."
It might be — we won't claim otherwise without proof. What we can show you is exactly how we validate our accuracy, methodology and all. Ask Bricklane for the same, and compare.

Do-Not-Say List

  • Don't call Bricklane's valuations "inaccurate" — there's no evidence for that, and it's an easy claim to get caught overstating.
  • Don't claim Bricklane has "no compliance features" in absolute terms — say specifically that their audit trail lacks comparable-level detail, which is verifiable.
  • Don't price-match or imply we'll come down to their price point — reframe to cost-of-an-indefensible-decision instead.
