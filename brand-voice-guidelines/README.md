# Brand Voice Guidelines

## The problem

Brand voice guidelines are usually a PDF nobody rereads after onboarding. In practice, voice consistency breaks down in the small, high-volume stuff — a support-team blog post, a sales one-pager written under deadline, a partner co-marketing email — not the flagship campaign copy that gets three rounds of review.

## The approach

A skill that encodes the voice as **rules with a reason**, not just adjectives ("confident but not salesy" is not actionable on its own), and applies them as a rewrite pass on existing drafts. It's deliberately structured to explain *why* it changed something, not just deliver a rewrite — so the person whose draft it is actually learns the pattern instead of treating it as a black box.

## Current limitations

Right now the rule set is condensed to what generalizes across content types. The fuller internal terminology doc (approved/banned product terms, how we refer to our own AI use) and a running library of examples pulled from top-performing published content are next to fold in.
