# Call Transcript — Fenwick & Oak Realty

**Date:** August 2026 · **Duration:** ~15 minutes · **Call type:** First discovery call
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Priya Chandra, Broker Team Lead, Fenwick & Oak Realty (independent brokerage, ~25 agents)

---

**Marcus:** Priya, thanks for the time. What's prompting you to look at valuation tools now specifically?

**Priya:** We're losing a couple of listing appointments a month, our agents think, to competitors who show up with sharper market data. It's not a crisis, but it adds up.

**Marcus:** Understood. Before we get into the product — how do your agents currently price a listing conversation?

**Priya:** Mostly gut feel plus whatever comps they can pull manually, which varies a lot by agent experience level. Our newer agents especially struggle.

**Marcus:** That's actually a good way to frame the value — less "replace what experienced agents do well" and more "give newer agents the floor that experienced agents already have in their head."

**Priya:** That's exactly the gap, yes. Okay, what's this going to cost per agent? I need to be direct about that because seat-based pricing across 25 agents adds up fast for a brokerage our size.

**Marcus:** Fair, and I'd rather give you real numbers than a vague range. It's seat-based, tiered by team size — at 25 agents you'd likely land in [a specific tier], which I can confirm precisely rather than estimate loosely.

**Priya:** Okay. And realistically, how long does it take an agent to actually get comfortable using this day to day? I don't want to pay for 25 seats and have half the team never actually adopt it.

**Marcus:** That's the right question to ask before the pricing question, honestly. I'll be straightforward: onboarding for someone comfortable with basic software tools is usually fast, same day for the core valuation pull. For agents less comfortable with new tools generally, it can take longer to build the habit — that's more about individual comfort with new software than the product's complexity, but I don't want to pretend everyone picks it up instantly.

**Priya:** That tracks with my team — I've got a wide range of comfort levels. Is there a lighter onboarding path for the less tech-comfortable half of my team specifically?

**Marcus:** That's a fair ask, and honestly not something I have a fully specific answer for today — most onboarding material assumes a baseline comfort level. Let me check with our customer success team on whether there's a simplified path for less tech-comfortable users, rather than assume there is one.

**Priya:** I'd appreciate that — it's the actual risk in this for me, more than the per-seat cost.

**Marcus:** Understood, I'll treat that as the real open question rather than pricing. Can I follow up by early next week with a real answer on onboarding support specifically for less tech-comfortable users, plus the exact pricing tier for your team size?

**Priya:** Yes, that works. If the onboarding story is solid, pricing at that tier is workable for us.

**Marcus:** Good to know pricing itself isn't the blocker if onboarding is handled well. I'll get back to you with both by early next week.
