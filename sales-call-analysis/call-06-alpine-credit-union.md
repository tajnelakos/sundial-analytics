# Call Transcript — Alpine Credit Union

**Date:** July 2026 · **Duration:** ~20 minutes · **Call type:** Third call, late-stage
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Heinrich Bauer, Head of Credit Risk, Alpine Credit Union (regional bank, DACH)

---

**Marcus:** Heinrich, good to talk again. Last time we left it that your team was doing internal diligence — where did that land?

**Heinrich:** Positively, mostly. The audit trail held up under scrutiny from two of my analysts, which is honestly the bar that mattered most to me. My concern now isn't the product — it's you as a company.

**Marcus:** Say more about that.

**Heinrich:** You're smaller than the vendors we've traditionally used. If Sundial were acquired, or ran into funding trouble, or simply didn't exist in three years, we'd have a valuation infrastructure dependency on a company that's gone. That's a real board-level question for us, not a formality.

**Marcus:** It's a legitimate one, and I'd rather engage with the substance than give you a reassurance-shaped non-answer. A few things I can actually offer: contractual data portability terms, so if the relationship ever ended, your historical valuation data and audit trails export in a usable format, not held hostage. That's something I can commit to in writing, not just say on a call.

**Heinrich:** That helps, actually — has that come up before, or is that new?

**Marcus:** It's a standard part of our enterprise contract, not something invented for this call. What I can't promise is that no vendor risk exists — that would be dishonest, every vendor at any size carries some. What I can point to is that our platform's architecture doesn't lock you in in a way that makes an exit catastrophic if it ever came to that.

**Heinrich:** That's a more honest answer than most vendors give when asked this directly. Can you share how many banks of a size comparable to ours are live on the platform today? Not a total customer count — comparable ones.

**Marcus:** I can get you a specific number rather than a vague "several" — let me confirm exact figures with our customer team so I'm not misquoting, and I'll follow up in writing today or tomorrow.

**Heinrich:** Appreciated. If that number is reasonable, I think we're in a position to move to contract discussion. I want to be direct: the product itself has cleared our bar. This is genuinely the last open question.

**Marcus:** That's good to hear, and I don't want to undersell the seriousness of your question by rushing past it — vendor stability at your size of relationship is exactly the kind of thing that should get real diligence, not a sales-call brush-off.

**Heinrich:** I appreciate that you're not just telling me what I want to hear. Get me the customer count and the data portability terms in writing, and I think we can move.

**Marcus:** I'll have both to you by end of week. Should I loop in anyone from our side on the contract terms discussion, or is that a separate conversation once you've reviewed?

**Heinrich:** Separate conversation, once I've reviewed what you send. But I expect this moves quickly from here.

**Marcus:** Understood — I'll prioritize getting you accurate numbers over getting you numbers fast, but I'll move quickly either way.
