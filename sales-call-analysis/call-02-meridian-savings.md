# Call Transcript — Meridian Savings

**Date:** March 2026 · **Duration:** ~20 minutes · **Call type:** Second call, post-demo
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Julia Hoffmann, VP of Mortgage Lending, Meridian Savings (regional bank, DACH)

---

**Marcus:** Julia, good to see you again. Last time we walked through the platform end to end — today I wanted to get into the specifics of what's actually blocking a decision, if anything is. Where's your head at?

**Julia:** Honestly, the product looks good. My team liked the workflow, the integration story is clean. The sticking point is Bricklane — we got a competing quote and it's meaningfully lower per call.

**Marcus:** How much lower, roughly, if you can share?

**Julia:** About 15%, give or take, at our expected volume.

**Marcus:** Okay, that's real money at your volume, I'm not going to pretend it isn't. Can I ask what your team thinks Bricklane gives up to hit that price?

**Julia:** That's actually the interesting part. Our risk team looked at their sample output and it's a confidence score, not a breakdown. My underwriters don't care about that day to day — they just want a number fast. But our Head of Credit Risk cares a lot, and she's not in this call by accident.

**Marcus:** So there's a real split inside the buying committee — speed-focused underwriting versus risk wanting defensibility.

**Julia:** Exactly. And I'll be candid, from a pure ops perspective, if it were only up to me, the price difference is hard to ignore. What's your answer to that, beyond "we're better"?

**Marcus:** Fair question, and I'll answer it directly rather than dodge it. We're not going to match Bricklane's price — if we did, we'd be making the same tradeoff they made to get there. What I'd ask you to do instead is quantify the other side of the ledger: what does it cost your team, in hours and in risk exposure, the first time a valuation gets questioned and nobody can explain how it was derived? That's not hypothetical for a bank your size — it's a when, not an if.

**Julia:** We haven't formally costed that out, no.

**Marcus:** Would it help if I sent over a rough framework your risk team could fill in themselves — hours per review, frequency, cost per hour — so it's your numbers, not our sales pitch?

**Julia:** Yes, that would actually help internally. My Head of Credit Risk would take that seriously coming from her own team's estimate rather than from you.

**Marcus:** I'll get that to you this week. Separately — has Bricklane shown you an actual audit trail, or just talked about pricing and speed?

**Julia:** Mostly pricing and speed, if I'm honest. Their rep was very good on the ROI numbers, less specific when my risk lead asked about explainability. I think that's when the temperature in the room changed a bit.

**Marcus:** That's consistent with what we generally see from them — strong on speed and cost, thinner once someone asks for comparable-level detail rather than a confidence score. I wouldn't take my word for it though — worth asking them directly for a real audit trail on a property your risk team picks, the same way you'd ask us.

**Julia:** We might do that, actually, just to be fair to both sides.

**Marcus:** That's the right instinct. One more thing worth asking either of us: what happens operationally if you need to explain a decision two years from now, after the person who ran the valuation has left the team? A confidence score doesn't age well for that. A comparable-level trail does.

**Julia:** That's a good point I hadn't considered. Okay — so, next step. I want to get the cost framework to my risk lead, and I think we need one more conversation with both her and our head of ops in the room, since this is really a two-stakeholder decision now.

**Marcus:** Makes sense. I'll send the framework by Thursday. Want me to propose some times for that combined call, or would you rather set it up on your side first?

**Julia:** Send times, I'll get it on the calendar. I think we're close here, honestly — this was the conversation I needed to have.

**Marcus:** Appreciate you being direct about the Bricklane comparison rather than letting it sit unspoken. Talk this week.
