# Call Transcript — Ironbridge Bank

**Date:** July 2026 · **Duration:** ~14 minutes · **Call type:** First discovery call
**Rep:** Anna Kowalski (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Fredrik Nilsson, Head of Credit Risk, Ironbridge Bank (regional lender)

---

**Anna:** Fredrik, thanks for the time. What's the context behind reaching out now?

**Fredrik:** I'll be honest, the timing is awkward — we're about four months into implementing ValuAI. This call is more due diligence on my part than an active evaluation.

**Anna:** Appreciate you being upfront about that rather than letting me think this is a live deal. Can I ask what's prompting due diligence mid-implementation — is something not going well, or is this just healthy vendor awareness?

**Fredrik:** A bit of both, honestly. Implementation's taken longer than we were told, and the audit trail feature — which was a big part of why we picked them — is technically live but our team's found it thinner in practice than the sales demo suggested. Fewer named comparables than expected on some property types.

**Anna:** That's useful and specific — do you know if that's consistent across property types, or worse in some than others?

**Fredrik:** Worse on commercial and mixed-use, from what my analysts have seen. Residential seems more solid.

**Anna:** Okay. I don't want to pile on a competitor you're already contracted with — that's not a useful use of this call. What would actually be useful to you right now?

**Fredrik:** Candidly, just knowing what my options are if this doesn't improve. I don't want to be back here in eight months having burned a year on a vendor switch that didn't pan out, with no sense of alternatives.

**Anna:** That's reasonable. I'd suggest we don't treat this as a sales conversation today — more like: I'll share what our audit trail actually looks like on commercial and mixed-use properties specifically, since that's your live pain point, and you can judge for yourself whether it's a meaningfully different depth of detail.

**Fredrik:** That would be useful, yes.

**Anna:** *(walks through commercial property example)* ...this is the comparable set and weighting for a commercial property specifically — I'd encourage you to compare this directly against what ValuAI actually produced for a similar property type, not from memory.

**Fredrik:** That is more detailed on the commercial side than what we've seen. I'm not going to make any decisions today, to be clear — we're contracted, and switching mid-implementation would be its own mess.

**Anna:** Understood, and I wouldn't push you to. What I'd suggest: keep this on file, and if the commercial/mixed-use gap doesn't improve over the next few months, reach back out — I'd rather be a known, low-pressure option than someone who pushed too hard at the wrong moment.

**Fredrik:** I appreciate that approach, honestly. I'll keep this on file and revisit if things don't improve.

**Anna:** Sounds good. No urgency from my side — just wanted you to have real information rather than nothing.
