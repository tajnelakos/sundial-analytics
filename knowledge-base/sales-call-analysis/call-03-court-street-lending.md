# Call Transcript — Court Street Lending

**Date:** August 2026 · **Duration:** ~19 minutes · **Call type:** Second call, competitive evaluation
**Rep:** Anna Kowalski (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** David Okafor, Head of Credit Risk, Court Street Lending (national lender, €5B+ annual origination)

*Context: flagged as a Tier 2 watch-list account (see [`icp-buying-signal-monitor`](../../sales-tools/icp-buying-signal-monitor/example.md)) after a senior risk hire; this is the resulting evaluation call, several months later.*

---

**Anna:** David, thanks for the time. Last we spoke, your team was early in scoping a valuation tooling refresh — sounds like things have moved since then.

**David:** They have. We shortlisted three vendors — you, ValuAI, and our current provider, who we're not planning to renew with. I'll be upfront: ValuAI's made the shortlist mainly because they just announced their audit trail feature went fully live. That was the thing we were told to wait on before.

**Anna:** That's a real development, and I'd rather engage with it directly than pretend it didn't happen. What have you seen of it so far — a demo, documentation, anything hands-on?

**David:** A demo, and it looked reasonable. Comparable-level detail, similar to what you showed us. Their rep was confident it's production-ready.

**Anna:** Okay. A few questions I'd genuinely want answered if I were in your seat, not because I doubt them reflexively, but because "just launched" and "production-proven" are different claims: how many customers are actually running it live today, not just have access to it? And is there a reference customer who'd speak specifically to the audit trail feature — not to ValuAI generally?

**David:** I hadn't pushed on that, no. Their rep mentioned "an enterprise customer" but didn't name one when I asked directly.

**Anna:** That's worth noting, not as a gotcha, just as a data point. Our audit trail's been in production through real regulated-lending deals for over a year at this point — it's not a claim that it exists, it's a track record of it holding up when an examiner actually pushes on it. I'd ask ValuAI the same question you'd ask us: can we see this run live, on a property your team picks, not a rehearsed one?

**David:** That's fair. We hadn't done that with either of you yet, actually — we've seen curated demos from both.

**Anna:** Then let's fix that on our side today, if you have a few extra minutes — do you have a real property in front of you, or details for one?

**David:** I can pull one up, give me a second... okay, I've got a commercial property from a deal we closed last quarter.

**Anna:** *(runs live audit trail)* ...so here's the comparable set, the weighting on each, and the confidence range — all specific to this property, not a template. Does this match what you'd need to show an examiner?

**David:** Yes, actually, this is more specific than what I expected. Okay — I'll ask ValuAI for the same thing before we go further with them. That's a reasonable bar to hold both of you to.

**Anna:** Appreciate you holding us to it too, for what it's worth — I'd rather earn this than win it by default because a competitor didn't get asked the same question.

**David:** Separately — implementation. You're a smaller company than ValuAI. Board's going to ask about vendor stability at our size of relationship.

**Anna:** That's a legitimate question at your scale, and I won't pretend company size doesn't matter to a board. What I'd point to: our audit-log format and integration approach were built around regulated lending from day one, not adapted from a general product after the fact — that's a different kind of stability question than headcount. Happy to provide references from institutions closer to your size and segment if that helps the board conversation specifically.

**David:** That would help, yes. Okay — I think the honest next step is I go push ValuAI on the same live-demo ask, then we reconvene. I don't want to short-circuit our own process by deciding today.

**Anna:** That's the right call, and I'd rather you run a fair process than rush it. Send me a time in a couple weeks once you've had that ValuAI conversation, and we can pick up from there.

**David:** Will do. This was a more useful call than I expected going in.
