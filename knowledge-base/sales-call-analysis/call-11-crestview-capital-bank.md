# Call Transcript — Crestview Capital Bank

**Date:** August 2026 · **Duration:** ~19 minutes · **Call type:** Second call, technical discovery
**Rep:** Anna Kowalski (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Ingrid Solberg, Head of Credit Risk, Crestview Capital Bank (regional lender)

---

**Anna:** Ingrid, good to continue from last time. You mentioned you wanted to go deeper on the technical and regulatory side today — where should we start?

**Ingrid:** Our market has a specific local certification requirement for automated valuation models — it's not universal across the region, just our specific national regulator. Any vendor we use needs to hold that certification, or be actively pursuing it with a credible timeline.

**Anna:** I want to be accurate rather than reassuring here — I don't know off the top of my head whether we currently hold that specific certification. Can you tell me the exact name of it so I follow up precisely rather than guess?

**Ingrid:** It's [specific regulatory certification], administered through our national banking authority.

**Anna:** Understood. I'll check with our compliance team today and get you a precise answer — whether we hold it, whether we're pursuing it, or whether it's not currently on our roadmap at all. I'd rather tell you "we don't have this and here's honestly where it stands" than waste your time with a vague maybe.

**Ingrid:** I appreciate that. To be clear, this isn't a dealbreaker by itself if you're actively pursuing it with a real timeline — my board would accept "in progress" if it's credible. What they won't accept is "we hadn't heard of this."

**Anna:** That's a useful distinction, and I'll make sure whatever I tell you reflects the actual state of things, not a hopeful spin on it. Separate from certification — walk me through what a valuation review looks like day to day for your team, so I understand the actual workflow this needs to fit into.

**Ingrid:** Sure. Our underwriters pull a valuation, our risk analysts spot-check a sample weekly, and anything flagged goes to a senior reviewer. The spot-check process is the most manual part — currently done by exporting to a spreadsheet and reviewing by hand.

**Anna:** That's useful — our platform has a review-queue feature that could replace the spreadsheet export step, flagging valuations for review based on criteria your team sets, rather than a blind weekly sample. Would that actually fit how your senior reviewers work, or would it change the process in a way that creates its own friction?

**Ingrid:** That could genuinely help, actually — right now the weekly sample is somewhat arbitrary, criteria-based flagging would be an improvement, not just a lateral change.

**Anna:** Good — I'll make sure that's shown clearly whenever we do a deeper technical walkthrough with your analysts, since it sounds like a real workflow win, not just a feature checkbox. Getting back to the certification question — is there a decision deadline I should be mindful of, so I know how urgently to chase the compliance answer?

**Ingrid:** No hard deadline yet, but I'd like an answer within the next couple of weeks so I know whether to keep evaluating you seriously or look elsewhere for that requirement specifically.

**Anna:** Understood — I'll prioritize getting you a precise, honest answer within that window rather than a rushed guess sooner.

**Ingrid:** That works. This was a useful technical conversation regardless of how the certification question lands.
