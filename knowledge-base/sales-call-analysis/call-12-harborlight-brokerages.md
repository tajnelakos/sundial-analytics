# Call Transcript — Harborlight Brokerages

**Date:** August 2026 · **Duration:** ~13 minutes · **Call type:** First discovery call
**Rep:** Marcus Webb (Sundial Analytics) · **Prospect:** Dana Whitfield, Broker Team Lead, Harborlight Brokerages (independent brokerage, ~60 agents)

---

**Marcus:** Dana, thanks for the time. What made you take this call?

**Dana:** Curiosity more than urgency, I'll be honest. We've used PropIQ for about two years across the whole team. It's not perfect, but it's embedded — agents know it, it's in their daily workflow.

**Marcus:** Given that, what's the actual motivation to even take a call like this?

**Dana:** A newer agent who came from another brokerage mentioned your platform and said the valuation detail was noticeably better. That planted a seed, but I want to be upfront — switching 60 agents off an embedded tool is a real undertaking, not something I'd do for a marginal improvement.

**Marcus:** That's a fair bar to set, and I'd rather be direct about what would and wouldn't justify that disruption. Can I ask what specifically that agent said was better?

**Dana:** Mostly the comparable detail — apparently more granular, better able to explain a price recommendation to a skeptical seller.

**Marcus:** That tracks with how we're built — the goal is giving an agent something they can actually walk a seller through, not just a number. But I want to be honest with you: is "better comparable detail" actually a top-3 problem for your team today, or is it a nice-to-have that one agent happened to notice?

**Dana:** Fair question. Honestly, no — adoption and consistency across 60 agents is my actual top problem, not the depth of any single tool's output. Half my team barely uses PropIQ's more advanced features as it is.

**Marcus:** Then I don't think a "our data is more detailed" pitch is the right conversation for you right now, and I'd rather tell you that than manufacture urgency that isn't real. If the real problem is inconsistent adoption of the tool you already have, switching platforms doesn't obviously fix that — it might just reset the adoption curve to zero and create a worse problem temporarily.

**Dana:** That's refreshingly candid for a sales call, I'll say that.

**Marcus:** I'd rather have a shorter, honest conversation than a longer one that leads nowhere real. Here's what I'd suggest instead: keep our details on file. If PropIQ's specific limitations ever become an actual blocker — not "one agent noticed something," but a real operational problem — that's the moment this conversation is worth having properly, likely alongside a broader adoption push regardless of which platform you land on.

**Dana:** That's a reasonable way to leave it. I'll keep you in mind if something changes structurally, not just anecdotally.

**Marcus:** Sounds good. No follow-up pressure from my side — genuinely just glad you had a clear-eyed read on your own actual priority.
