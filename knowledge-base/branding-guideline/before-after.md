# Before / after

Draft for Sundial Analytics, rewritten per the [voice skill](./skill.md).

---

## Before

> In today's fast-changing real estate lending landscape, financial institutions are increasingly turning to advanced technology solutions to streamline their valuation processes. Our platform is powered by cutting-edge AI algorithms that deliver significantly faster results than traditional methods, helping users eliminate lending risk and transform their credit operations. Users receive a valuation output within a short timeframe, backed by industry-leading accuracy.

## After

> Manual property valuations slow down lending decisions and are hard to defend when an auditor asks how you got the number. Sundial gives you a valuation in minutes, with a full audit trail showing which comparables were used and how they were weighted — so you're not just fast, you're able to explain the decision.
>
> It won't eliminate lending risk on its own. What it does is cut the manual review time on valuations and give you a documented basis for the ones that get questioned.

## Changes and why

- Cut the opening ("In today's fast-changing...") — Rule 1. It's a throat-clearing setup that delays the actual claim by a full sentence.
- Removed "powered by cutting-edge AI algorithms" — Rule 3. This audience is explicitly skeptical of unexplained AI claims; replaced with a description of what the audit trail actually shows.
- Removed "significantly faster" — Rule 2. No number was available in the source draft, so softened to a defensible comparison ("valuation in minutes") instead of inventing a stat.
- Removed "eliminate lending risk" — Rule 6, overclaim. Replaced with what the product actually supports: cutting review time and providing a documented basis, not risk elimination.
- Switched to second person, active voice throughout — Rule 4 ("Users receive..." → "Sundial gives you...").
- Split into two short paragraphs, one idea each — Rule 5 (speed/audit trail vs. the honest limitation).
