Key findings

ValuAI: 0 wins in 8 head-to-head deals this period
Every deal where ValuAI was the named competitor was lost — a small sample, but a stark one, and consistent with positioning.md's warning that the explainability wedge is narrowing faster than expected since ValuAI's July 2026 GA launch. Their average quoted price (€94,626) also came in below our average deal size in those deals (€103,344) — they are no longer only a capability threat, price is now part of their play too. This is the single most urgent finding in this analysis.
We lose the bigger deals more often than the smaller ones
Average lost-deal size (€88,435) is larger than average won-deal size (€72,464), and Tier 2 (larger, more procurement layers — see icp.md) wins at only 33.3% against Tier 1's 37% and Tier 3's 52.8%. Deal size and procurement complexity appear to move together against us, not deal size alone.
Response time correlates with outcome; demo quality doesn't
Won deals averaged a 30.4-hour first response versus 37.1 hours for lost deals — a real, actionable gap. Demo quality scored identically for wins and losses (3.83 average either way), suggesting the demo itself isn't where deals are being decided — speed to engage is.
27.6% of losses have no product cause at all
The single largest loss-feedback category is process/relationship/timing related, not a missing feature or a lost technical comparison — see Product Feedback below. Worth remembering before assuming every loss is a product gap to close.
