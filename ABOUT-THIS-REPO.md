# About this repo

This is a portfolio project, built to show what applied, structured AI usage looks like in a product marketing role — not a scraped or leaked company repo.

## Why a fictional company

The skills, prompts, and knowledge-base structure here are representative of real work; the company they're built around is not. Publishing my actual employer's competitive intelligence, deal data, or brand guidelines wouldn't be appropriate — so everything runs against **Sundial Analytics**, an invented B2B SaaS company (see [`knowledge-base.md`](./knowledge-base.md)), with invented competitors, personas, deals, and transcripts. No real company, competitor, customer, or employer data appears anywhere in this repo.

## What's real and what isn't

- **Real:** the approach — how each skill is structured, what it deliberately does and doesn't do, the reasoning behind the instructions, the shared knowledge-base architecture, the way the use cases connect to each other.
- **Not real:** Sundial Analytics itself, its competitors, its customers, every transcript, every deal, every quoted figure.

Some skills are also shown with details generalized rather than as a finished, ready-to-run internal playbook — the goal is to demonstrate the quality and structure of the thinking, not to hand over a complete production system.

## Why it's built this way

Rather than a list of prompts, this repo is structured the way I'd actually want these systems to exist at a real company: a shared knowledge base ([`knowledge-base.md`](./knowledge-base.md), `icp.md`, `personas.md`, `positioning.md`, `voice-guide.md`, `competitive-landscape.md`) that every individual skill draws on instead of re-deriving customer and competitive context each time, plus an [`INSTRUCTIONS.md`](./INSTRUCTIONS.md) that routes a given task to the right tool.
