Company Overview & Strategy
Estemate is the long-standing incumbent in the France/Benelux market, built on two decades of relationship tenure and one of the region's largest historical valuation datasets. They position on trust and data depth, betting that switching costs and "nobody gets fired for choosing the incumbent" outweigh any product gap.
Positioning Framework
They say
"Two decades of trusted valuation data — the partner banks across the region have relied on for years."
Reality
Dated UI, slow release cadence, and publicly documented support responsiveness issues. (Source: 2 confirmed G2 reviews.) The relationship is real — the product experience hasn't kept pace with it.
We win because
A modern integration experience and faster implementation timeline, without asking anyone to gamble on an unproven vendor — we can point to what's actually slow today, not just claim to be newer.

Objection Handlers
"We've used Estemate for 10 years."
That history is worth respecting — this isn't about ripping out something that's worked. It's worth asking what's changed in that decade, both in what you need and in what they've actually shipped.
"Estemate has more historical data."
In this market, yes — that's real. The question is whether more historical data matters more than an audit trail an examiner will accept today. Those are different problems.
"Switching feels risky."
Fair, and we'd rather you feel that risk out loud than swallow it silently. What specifically feels risky — data migration, workflow disruption, something else? Let's look at that directly instead of the relationship in the abstract.

Do-Not-Say List

  • Don't call Estemate "outdated" as a blanket claim — it reads as disrespecting the buyer's own long-standing choice.
  • Don't disparage the relationship itself — the switching-cost concern is legitimate, not irrational.
  • Don't claim their data quality is worse — the documented weakness is UI, support, and release cadence, not data accuracy.
